# Licensing

**DataArc.EntityFrameworkCore is available free of charge.**

Permitted use of DataArc.EntityFrameworkCore does not require:

- a commercial DataArc license;
- a DataArc license key;
- runtime activation;
- per-developer fees;
- runtime fees; or
- a time-limited trial.

The package can be used in personal, commercial, educational, government, and enterprise applications subject to the package License Agreement.

## Commercial DataArc products

DataArc.EntityFrameworkCore may be used alongside commercially licensed DataArc products.

The **DataArc Orchestration Framework** is a separate commercial product for applications that require broader orchestration, coordination, and database tooling capabilities.

Installing or using DataArc.EntityFrameworkCore does not grant rights to commercially licensed DataArc products or functionality.

Conversely, use of DataArc.EntityFrameworkCore does not require purchase or activation of the DataArc Orchestration Framework.
